# Atlas – Privacy Policy

Atlas is a private personal assistant app used only by its owner.

- Atlas reads Gmail messages only to read and summarize them for its owner on his own iPhone.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- Email content is processed on the device and sent only to the AI provider the owner chose, only when he asks.
- The owner can disconnect Gmail at any time in Atlas settings or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
